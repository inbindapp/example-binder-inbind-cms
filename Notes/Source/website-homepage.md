It doesn't have to be this way. Most of these problems come down to one thing: your content tools weren't built with you in mind. Inbind is.

## How it Works (3 steps)

1. **Connect your site** — Hook up your Webflow site, Webstudio project, or framework of choice. Your existing content comes along for the ride.
2. **Edit in one workspace** — All your collections, fields, and content in one place. A clean editor with SEO fields front and center. No jumping between tools.
3. **Hit publish when you're ready** — Review your changes and push content live. No need to touch code. No Designer access needed. No deploy step. Just publish.

## Connections / Platforms

**Heading:** One CMS. Pick your platform.

Inbind CMS connects to the tools your team already uses. You handle the content. Your developers handle the frontend. Everyone stays in their lane.

- **Webflow** — Faster editing, unlimited collections, and publishing without opening Designer.
- **Webstudio** — A purpose-built CMS for your Webstudio projects. Familiar editing, zero lock-in.
- **Next.js** — Structured content your Next.js app can fetch and render. You manage the words, not the code.
- **Astro** — A CMS that matches Astro's content-first philosophy. Clean data, fast publishing.
- **Nuxt** — Structured collections and SEO tools for your Nuxt project. No added complexity.
- **SvelteKit** — A familiar editing experience with the extras your content team needs.

## Capabilities / Features

**Heading:** Tools that actually help you publish better content. Less busywork, more publishing.

- **See everything at a glance** — Every collection item in one sortable table. Spot missing fields, check what's ready, and stop clicking into items one by one.
- **Edit with SEO built in** — Metadata, structure, and rich text in a clean editor. You'll actually want to fill in your SEO fields when they're right there in front of you.
- **Automate the repetitive stuff** — Use generated fields to compose SEO titles, descriptions, or any other field from your existing data. Set it once and stop copy-pasting.
- **Keep internal notes out of your published content** — Add status, owner, and notes fields that only your team can see. Track your workflow without cluttering what goes live on your site.
- **Catch problems before they go live** — Content health checks flag missing or incomplete fields before you publish. No more "oops, the meta description was empty" moments.
- **Publish without a developer** — Push content live straight from Inbind. No Designer access, no deploy pipelines, no waiting on someone else to hit the button.
