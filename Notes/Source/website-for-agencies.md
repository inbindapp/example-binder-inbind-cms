---

## Hero

**Headline:** Let clients publish safely while you stay in control.

**Subhead:** Inbind CMS is the fast, focused workspace for editing and publishing Webflow CMS content built for agencies and client teams that need speed, consistency, and fewer risks.

---

## Pain / Problem

**Heading:** Clients need access, not accidents.

When clients can't confidently edit their own content, you become the bottleneck.

You've built a solid Webflow site with carefully structured collections. But here's the problem: what makes sense to you can feel like a minefield to clients. One wrong click and they've broken something. So they either freeze up and wait for you, or forge ahead and create cleanup work.

---

## Value proposition for agencies

**A Content-First Workspace for Webflow** — Reduce context switching and keep agency and client teams aligned in one content operations workspace that mirrors Webflow CMS.

- **Easy to view table** — A table view for complete visibility of all fields within a collection.
- **SEO focused editor** — An SEO focused editor that treats metadata, structure, and discoverability as core features.
- **Manage fields** — A flexible field manager with generated fields to create new fields freely.

---

## Benefits

**Heading:** Consistent Content at Scale

Keep tone, structure, and metadata consistent across every client page without putting design and layouts at risk.

- **Ship updates in half the time** — Clients and writers edit fields and rich text quickly in an SEO-focused editor. Generated fields help scale programmatic pages fast.
- **See everything, miss nothing** — Use the table view to spot missing values and readiness at a glance so every collection item is ready to publish.
- **Organize your work with internal fields** — Add new fields in Inbind to manage categories and statuses without cluttering your Webflow fields.
- **Keep tabs on your content health** — Ensure your content is SEO ready with a simple check that your collection item meets the criteria for healthy content.
