## Pain / Problem

**Heading:** You love Webflow. Your CMS workflow? Not so much.

Webflow handles design. Inbind handles content. Together, they give you a full website workflow.

Webflow is great for designing. But day-to-day content work — editing fields, updating rich text, managing SEO metadata across dozens of collection items — the built-in CMS can feel slow and limiting. Your content team shouldn't need to open Designer for every update. And they shouldn't be held back by collection limits or missing workflow tools. That's where Inbind CMS comes in.

---

## Core capabilities

- **Everything in one workspace** — All your Webflow collections, fields, and content in one clean editing workspace.
- **See everything at a glance** — Every collection item in one sortable table. Spot missing fields, check what's ready, and stop clicking into items one by one.
- **Edit with SEO built in** — Metadata, structure, and rich text in a clean editor. You'll actually want to fill in your SEO fields when they're right there in front of you.
- **Create fields your way** — A flexible field manager with generated fields. Build the content structure you need, freely.

---

## Extra features vs. Webflow's native CMS

- **Unlimited collections, no pricing tiers** — Webflow limits collections based on your plan. Inbind CMS doesn't. Create as many internal collections as you need without worrying about hitting caps or upgrading.
- **Automate the repetitive stuff** — Use generated fields to compose SEO titles, descriptions, or any other field from your existing data. Set it once and stop copy-pasting.
- **Keep internal notes out of your published content** — Content health checks flag missing or incomplete fields before you publish. No more going live with empty metadata.
- **Catch problems before they go live** — Content health checks flag missing or incomplete fields before you publish. No more "oops, the meta description was empty" moments.
- **Tables in rich text** — Add tables to your long-form content. Rendered as standard HTML and styled with your existing Webflow CSS.
- **Publish without a developer** — Push content live straight from Inbind. No Designer access, no waiting on someone else to hit the button.
