# Mission & Vision

> Answer the questions below to articulate why your company exists and where it's headed.

## Mission

Help content marketers spend their time on content — not on their CMS.

## Tagline

Content Operations

*Source: Founder interview, July 2026*

## Mission statement

*(Not explicitly published. Inferred from website copy.)*

Inbind exists so content marketers can focus on the work that actually matters. Publishing should be simple. SEO should be built into the workflow. Developers shouldn't be the bottleneck between a draft and a live page.

## Vision

*(Not explicitly published. Inferred from product direction.)*

A world where content teams have their own purpose-built tooling — as powerful for content marketers as design tools are for designers, without the dependency on developers for day-to-day publishing work.

## Vision statement

An opinionated, AI-powered platform covering the full content operations lifecycle — from brief or idea through to published article, tweet, post, or video. Inbind handles the "How" of content marketing so teams can focus on the "What".

*Source: Founder interview, July 2026*

## What you're optimizing for

Speed and quality of content publishing, without tradeoffs. Removing friction from the content-to-live workflow. SEO completeness at scale.

## How do you measure whether you're living up to your mission?

Not documented. Likely proxies: time-to-publish, rate of complete SEO metadata across published items, number of publishes that required zero developer involvement.
