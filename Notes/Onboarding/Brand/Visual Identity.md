## How would you describe your visual style? (e.g., minimalist, bold, organic, technical)

Sunny, warm, and clean. The brand is described internally as "a sunny day in Spain/Portugal" — open, bright, optimistic, and chill. Functional rather than flashy. The Volkswagen Golf is used as a reference point: practical, does what it's supposed to, no need to be flashy.

*Source: Notion export — Saturday at Petteri's.md, Brand.md*

## What imagery or photography style fits the brand?

Visual motifs from the brand document:
- **Juices** — "Get the creative juices flowing", "Juice worth the squeeze"
- **Flowing waves**
- **Tropical fruits** / freshly squeezed fruit juices
- **Rays of sunshine**

Real people in authentic moments preferred; avoid stock photography.

*Source: Notion export — Brand.md*

## What visual styles or aesthetics should be avoided?

- Corporate, heavy, or dark aesthetics
- Engineering-led / technical UI aesthetics (conflicts with the Everyman brand positioning)
- Flashy or luxury visual language (inconsistent with the functional, understated approach)
