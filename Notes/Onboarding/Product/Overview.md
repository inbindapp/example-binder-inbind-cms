# Product Overview

> Answer the questions below to describe what your product is at a high level.

## What is the product called?

Inbind CMS

## What does it do in one sentence?

Inbind CMS is a content management workspace that lets content marketers create, edit, and publish content across Webflow, Webstudio, Next.js, Astro, Nuxt, and SvelteKit — all from one place.

## Who is it built for?

Content marketers, solo marketers, small content teams, and agencies managing Webflow CMS content (and other frontend frameworks). Also used by agencies handing off content editing to clients.

## What is the core job it does for the user?

Removes the friction between drafting content and publishing it live. Users get a clean, focused editor with SEO fields front and center, a table view across all collection items, and direct publish — no developer needed, no Designer access required.

## What does the user experience look like end to end?

1. Connect your site (Webflow, Webstudio, or a static framework via S3-compatible storage)
2. Your existing collections and content are synced automatically
3. Edit content in one clean workspace — rich text, SEO metadata, all fields in one view
4. Publish directly from Inbind — no deploy step, no opening Webflow Designer

## What stage is the product at? (e.g., idea, beta, live, scaling)

Live. Available at cms.inbind.app. Actively acquiring customers. 14-day free trial, no credit card required.
