# Product Vision

> Answer the questions below to articulate where the product is ultimately headed.

## What is the world you want to create through this product?

A world where content marketers spend their time on content — not on their tools. Publishing should be as simple as writing. SEO should be built into the workflow, not bolted on. Developers shouldn't be the bottleneck between a draft and a live page.

## What does the product look like at full scale — in 3–5 years?

An opinionated, AI-powered platform that covers content operations from brief or idea through to published article, tweet, post, or video. The "How" of content marketing is handled by Inbind — operations, publishing, SEO, distribution — so marketers can focus on the "What" (strategy, quality, ideas), with AI support even there.

*Source: Founder interview, July 2026*

## What does success look like for your users at that point?

Content teams ship faster, with fewer errors, and without depending on a developer for every update. Every published piece is SEO-complete. Agencies can give clients safe, independent access to their content.

## What is the bigger bet underlying the product?

That content teams have been underserved by tools built for developers and designers — and that a CMS built specifically for content marketers, with SEO and workflow at its core, will win that segment.

## What would have to be true for the vision to be achieved?

- Webflow (and other frontend platforms) remain popular and continue not fully solving the content marketer's workflow
- SEO and AEO continue to matter as discovery channels
- Content teams grow in influence and demand their own purpose-built tooling
