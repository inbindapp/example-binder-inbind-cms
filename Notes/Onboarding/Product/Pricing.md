# Pricing

> Answer the questions below to document your pricing model — useful for content that references plans, value, or ROI.

## What is your pricing model?

Flat monthly fee per workspace. One price, no per-seat charges.

## What are your pricing tiers?

**Pro — $29/month per workspace**
- 14-day free trial included
- No credit card required to start
- Cancel anytime

## Who is each tier designed for?

The Pro plan is the only current tier. Designed for content teams of any size — solo marketers up to agencies managing client sites.

## What is the rationale behind your pricing?

Per-workspace, not per-seat, so teams aren't penalized for adding collaborators. Positioning copy: "One workspace, one price. No per-seat surprises."

## Is there a free trial or freemium option? What does it include?

14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. Trial appears to include full Pro features (not documented otherwise).

## What objections do customers raise about pricing, and how do you respond?

Not documented at time of fetch.

## What triggers an upgrade from one tier to the next?

Currently one paid tier. No documented upgrade path beyond starting a trial and converting to Pro.
