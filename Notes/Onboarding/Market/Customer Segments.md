# Target Audience

> Answer the questions below to build a clear picture of who you're serving.

## Who is your primary target audience?

Content marketers and content teams who manage website content on Webflow (or other frontend frameworks), and who are slowed down by the friction between their content tools and their publishing workflow.

## What are their key demographics? (age, role, industry, company size, location, etc.)

- **Role:** Content Marketer, Content Manager, SEO Manager, Marketing Manager, Agency Account Manager
- **Industry:** Tech, SaaS, agencies, any content-heavy business
- **Company size:** Startups to mid-size (solo marketers up to small teams; agencies managing multiple client sites)
- **Technical level:** Non-technical — no coding, no Webflow Designer access expected

## What are their main pain points or frustrations?

- Jumping between tools to draft, add metadata, and publish
- Needing a developer to push content live
- Webflow's CMS editor feeling slow for day-to-day updates
- Forgetting to fill in SEO fields before publishing
- No visibility across all collection items at once
- Collection limits based on Webflow plan tier

## What are their goals and motivations?

- Publish content faster without sacrificing quality or SEO completeness
- Ship updates independently without depending on developers or designers
- Keep content consistent across many collection items
- Scale content volume (including programmatic SEO pages)
- Give clients safe, easy editing without risking layout damage

## What does their day-to-day look like, and where does your product fit in?

They spend time in docs and drafts tools, then switch to Webflow CMS to copy in content and fill fields, then either publish themselves (if they have access) or ping a developer. Inbind collapses this into one workspace — write, fill SEO fields, and publish all in one place.

## What do they read, watch, or follow? (channels, publications, influencers)

Not documented. Likely follows SEO and content marketing communities, Webflow's ecosystem channels, and marketing newsletters.

## What objections or hesitations do they have before buying?

- "We already use Webflow — why add a tool on top?"
- "Will this break our site or Webflow setup?"
- "Is $29/month worth it for content editing?"
- "How long does setup take?"

## Describe your ideal customer in a short paragraph:

A content marketer at a Webflow-powered company who publishes blog posts, case studies, or SEO pages regularly. They're confident in their content skills but frustrated by the tools — too many tabs, too much waiting on developers, too many posts going live with empty metadata. They want to own the full publishing workflow end-to-end, without touching code or Designer.

---

## Secondary segments

**Agencies** — Digital agencies building Webflow sites for clients. They want to hand off content editing to clients safely, without risking layout damage or fielding support tickets every time the client tries to update a page. Inbind gives clients a focused, safe editing environment.
