# Market Trends

> Answer the questions below to capture the forces shaping your market — useful context for timely, relevant content.

## What are the major trends currently shaping your market?

- AI tools are accelerating content drafting velocity but not solving the publishing workflow — the bottleneck has moved downstream
- AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is emerging alongside SEO as a content discovery channel, raising the bar for content structure and completeness
- Programmatic SEO is becoming a standard tactic for scaling organic content, creating demand for CMS tooling that can support source-to-destination content generation at scale
- Content teams are growing in influence and pushing back against developer-dependent publishing workflows

*Source: [Website for Marketers](Notes/Source/website-for-marketers.md), [Pricing Page](Notes/Source/website-pricing.md), [Website Homepage](Notes/Source/website-homepage.md)*

## What technological shifts are relevant to your space?

- AI content generation tools (drafting is faster; the editing, SEO, and publishing workflow is unchanged)
- Headless and composable CMS architecture becoming mainstream (enabling tools like Inbind to layer on top of existing frontends without requiring migration)
- S3-compatible object storage (Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2) becoming cheap and accessible, enabling JSON-based content delivery for any framework

*Source: [Docs — Connections](Notes/Source/docs-connections.md), [Website for Marketers](Notes/Source/website-for-marketers.md)*

## What behavioral or cultural changes are affecting your customers?

Content teams are being pushed to produce more, faster — but their expectations of the tools they use are also rising. They're comfortable adopting new SaaS tools ($50–200/month per tool is a normal agency budget). They want Notion-like editing experiences in their CMS. They're increasingly unwilling to be dependent on developers for day-to-day publishing.
