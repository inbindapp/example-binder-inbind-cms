# Positioning

> Answer the questions below to define how your product sits in the market.

## What category does your product belong to?

Content Management System (CMS) — specifically a content operations workspace built for content marketers, layered on top of frontend platforms rather than replacing them.

## Who is the target customer?

Content marketers and content teams managing website content on Webflow (primarily) or other frontend frameworks (Webstudio, Astro, Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit).

## What is the key benefit you deliver?

One clean workspace to edit, review, and publish content — with SEO built in, no developer required to publish, and full visibility across all collection items.

## Who are you positioning against? (competitors or alternatives)

- **Webflow's native CMS editor** — the most common alternative (status quo). Slow for content work, no SEO-focused editor, collection limits based on plan, requires Designer access to publish.
- **Other headless CMS tools** (Contentful, Sanity, etc.) — more developer-centric, complex to set up, not designed around the content marketer's workflow.
- **The three-tab workflow** — drafting in Google Docs, metadata in a spreadsheet, publishing via a developer. The real incumbent.

## What makes you different or better than those alternatives?

vs. Webflow's native CMS:
- SEO fields front and center in the editor
- Publish without opening Designer
- Table view across all items
- Content health checks
- Generated fields and programmatic SEO
- Unlimited collections (no plan-based limits)
- Internal fields that don't sync to Webflow
- Bidirectional sync (edits in either tool stay in sync)

vs. headless CMS tools:
- Built for content marketers, not developers
- Connects to the platform you already use — no migration required
- Setup takes minutes (connects to existing Webflow collections automatically)

## Fill in the positioning statement:

For content marketers and content teams who manage website content on Webflow (and other frontend frameworks), Inbind CMS is a content operations workspace that lets them edit, review, and publish content without developer support — unlike Webflow's native CMS editor, which is slow, lacks SEO tooling, and requires Designer access to publish.

## How do you want to be perceived in 3 words?

Fast. Focused. Content-first.
