# Market

Inbind's primary market is content marketers at Webflow-powered companies — people who are confident in their craft but slowed by tools that weren't designed for them. The positioning is squarely against Webflow's native CMS editor (the status quo) and the developer-centric headless CMS category. As of early 2026, the team is shifting its go-to-market focus from agencies toward direct content manager outreach. The competitive landscape has been mapped and includes Webflow sync tools, headless CMS platforms, AI content generators, and an emerging AI Visibility category.
