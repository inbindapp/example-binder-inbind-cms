## Long description

Inbind CMS is a content management workspace built for content marketers. Spend your time on content, not on your CMS — you shouldn't need three tabs open just to update a blog post, or a developer on standby to fix a missing SEO field. Inbind gives you one clean workspace to edit, review, and publish your content, with SEO fields front and center and direct publishing built in. It connects to the platforms your team already uses: Webflow, Webstudio, Next.js, Astro, Nuxt, and SvelteKit. Every collection item is visible in one sortable table — spot missing fields and check readiness at a glance. Use generated fields to automate repetitive metadata like SEO titles, descriptions, and reading time. Then publish straight from Inbind — no Designer access, no deploy pipeline, no waiting on a developer.
