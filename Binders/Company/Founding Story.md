# Founding Story

## Origin

Petteri was managing Webflow CMS at the company he worked at. Beyond the general UX friction of Webflow's editor, the breaking point was programmatic SEO: to make it work, he had to embed HTML tags directly into Webflow Designer, build the SEO content structure in Google Sheets, then export and import into Webflow CMS — a painful, fragile multi-step setup. The first thing they built was the table view: a direct copy of the Google Sheet he was already using to manage collection content.

## The problem

The gap between what content teams need to do (create and publish content at scale, with proper SEO) and what Webflow's CMS actually lets them do without developer workarounds. Petteri lived this pain directly — not as a hypothesis, but as his day job.

## The insight

The Google Sheet was already doing the job — a table of collection items, all fields visible, easy to scan and edit. The insight was to bring that into a proper product and connect it directly to the CMS, cutting out the export/import loop entirely.
